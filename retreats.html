The Grounded Hero Program

A sanctuary for first responders to heal, reflect, and build resilience. This is a space to decompress from the daily trauma of the front lines and reconnect in a natural, restorative environment.

A Proactive Approach to Mental Wellness

The Grounded Hero program is not therapy; it's a proactive, peer-supported wellness curriculum designed to equip you with tools for long-term resilience. Our courses focus on mitigating the effects of burnout and post-traumatic stress in a confidential and supportive setting.

We provide a tranquil space where you can disconnect from the stress of the job and reconnect with yourself and your peers.

Wellness Workshops

Led by experienced facilitators, our workshops cover topics like stress management, mindfulness, and healthy coping mechanisms.

Peer Support & Connection

Connect with fellow first responders who understand the unique challenges of the job. Share experiences and build a network of support.

Restorative Activities

Engage in low-stress activities like hiking, fishing, or simply enjoying the peace and quiet of the farm.
