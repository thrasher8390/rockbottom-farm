Lend a Hand, Grow Hope

Our volunteers are the lifeblood of our mission. By giving your time, you directly help us harvest food for hungry children and maintain a place of peace for our community's heroes. No farming experience is necessary—just a willingness to help.

How You Can Help

Farm Hands

Get your hands dirty! Help with planting, weeding, and harvesting our crops. This is where the magic happens, and every hand makes a difference.

Distribution Support

Assist in sorting, packing, and preparing our fresh produce for delivery to local food banks and community partners.

Event & Program Crew

Support our first responder retreats and community events. Help with setup, hospitality, and ensuring our guests feel welcome.
